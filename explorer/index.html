Geometry & calculation scope

The tube, jacket, crawl space, hub and farm envelopes use system-model dimensions. Floors and layers in the section are schematic. Cable, lattice, radiator and array arrangements illustrate the architecture; their fittings and layout are not construction drawings.

The array has the calculated stack area, drawn as one Sun-facing square beyond the hub, with its normal in the orbital plane. Radiators show their available radial reach, not a thermally sized panel layout. The jacket is drawn uniformly; the mass calculation includes the floor fill’s shielding credit.

Heat and inertia figures below are the inexpensive system-model bounds. Flexible dynamics, radiation transport, assembled thermal exchange and dated shipping studies are not rerun by this viewer.
